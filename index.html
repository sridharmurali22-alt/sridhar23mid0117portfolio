02 • QUESTIONS & CONCEPTS

NLP Questions Explored

Concept explanations for the topics studied; not verbatim transcripts of classroom questions.

MORPHOLOGY

How does morphology help NLP?

It studies how words are formed from meaningful units such as roots and affixes, supporting preprocessing and morphological analysis.

DEEPSHIELD AI

Does Whisper detect deepfakes?

No. Whisper transcribes speech; acoustic and linguistic evidence is assessed separately before the decision-fusion step.

WORD EMBEDDINGS

Why turn words into vectors?

Embeddings map text to numerical representations, allowing models to compare relationships and semantic similarity. Multilingual embeddings can support Indian languages.

PROJECT REVIEW

Why combine acoustic and linguistic signals?

The channels capture different types of evidence. Combining available signals can provide richer explanations, but requires careful validation and uncertainty handling.

07 • COURSE PROJECT • MULTIMODAL AUDIO ANALYSIS

DeepShield AI

Hybrid deepfake audio detection with spectrogram analysis and NLP-based transcript verification.

Audio forensicsEfficientNet-B0Whisper tinyspaCySentence-Transformers
🛡️
EXPLAINABLE AI

DeepShield AI analyzes audio through a 128-bin log-Mel spectrogram and EfficientNet-B0, transcribes speech with Whisper tiny, evaluates linguistic indicators with spaCy and Sentence-Transformers, and combines available evidence using a decision-fusion layer. The dashboard is designed to present the analysis in an explainable form.

Important distinction: Whisper is the speech-recognition component; it is not itself a deepfake detector. This portfolio illustrates system design. It does not execute the trained detection models in the browser.

System pipeline

Two evidence streams, then a fusion-and-explanation stage.

🎙️
1. Audio Input

Upload, validate and preprocess audio

🌊
2. Acoustic Path

128-bin log-Mel spectrogram → EfficientNet-B0

📝
3. Speech + NLP

Whisper tiny → spaCy + Sentence-Transformers

⚖️
4. Evidence Fusion

Combine available cues; reference-based style check if available

🔎
5. Explanation

Show individual signals and decision rationale

Key learning points

  • ✓ Audio upload and preprocessing
  • ✓ 128-bin log-Mel spectrogram
  • ✓ EfficientNet-B0 acoustic analysis
  • ✓ Whisper tiny speech transcription
  • ✓ spaCy and Sentence-Transformers NLP analysis
  • ✓ Speaker-style consistency, if reference data is provided
